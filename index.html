Research Activities

Wearable devices · Time series · Biomedical signals

Bacco — A Tool for Holistic Monitoring

An iOS application for holistic self-monitoring that builds a daily wellness index from individual behaviors and physiological parameters, giving users an intuitive picture of their overall well-being.

Stress Detection via Foundation Models and Federated Learning

An ongoing research project combining foundation models with federated learning to detect stress from wearable sensor data — preserving user privacy by keeping raw data on-device while training a shared global model.

Zenit — The Schedule That Cares

A stress-aware scheduling application. My contribution was a forecasting pipeline that predicts stress trends from Garmin device data, helping users plan their day around their energy levels.
