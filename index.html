Signals in. States out.

I'm Angelo, a biomedical engineer who turns physiological signals from wearables, from heartbeats on the wrist to brainwaves in the ear, into models of how people feel.

The body is constantly reporting on the mind. A racing heart, a quieter alpha rhythm, a little more sweat before a deadline. My work is teaching machines to read those reports reliably, privately, and in the real world.

Now · Zurich, Switzerland

Master's thesis at IDUN Technologies.

IDUN is an ETH Zurich spin-off building in-ear EEG: brain sensing from inside the ear canal. I do the data science behind their proprietary algorithms for estimating mental state from brain signals.

  • In-ear EEG

    Brain activity recorded where earbuds already sit. No cap, no gel.

  • Mental-state algorithms

    Models that map neural activity to cognitive and affective states.

  • Data science

    Signal processing, feature engineering and rigorous validation on real-world recordings.

01 / 03 · WRIST

The heart keeps a diary.

Optical and electrical sensors on the wrist capture every beat. From the intervals between beats I extract heart-rate variability (SDNN, RMSSD), the autonomic nervous system's most honest signature.

Listening from the ear.

EEG recorded in the ear canal reveals rhythms (theta, alpha, beta) that shift with attention, relaxation and cognitive load. The brain, measured with earbuds.

Published · IEEE EMBC 2026

A mental-stress index from a physiological foundation model.

I fine-tuned NormWear, a foundation model for wearable time series, on laboratory data, and added a regression head that predicts stress levels against validated psychometric questionnaires. The architecture is designed for federated deployment, so raw data never has to leave the device.

MIMIC-IV

Clinical phenotypes from ICU time series.

Variational autoencoders compress high-dimensional physiological recordings from the MIMIC-IV database into embeddings. Unsupervised clustering then reveals distinct clinical phenotypes.

VAEClusteringRepresentation learning

BACCO: holistic wellness monitoring.

An iOS app that builds a daily wellness index from HRV metrics (SDNN, RMSSD), physical activity and dietary habits. Built in Swift, synced through HealthKit.

About me

Hi, I'm Angelo.
Mostly harmless.

I trained at Politecnico di Milano and I'm now in Zurich, where the trains run on time and I'm trying hard to do the same with my thesis.
