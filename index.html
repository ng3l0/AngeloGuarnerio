Biomedical Engineer & Data Scientist

I connect physiological signals to mental states, applying machine learning to data from wearables, from the wrist to the brain.

I'm writing my MSc thesis at IDUN Technologies, an ETH Zurich spin-off, doing data science on proprietary algorithms that estimate mental state from in-ear EEG. Since 2024 I've also been a student researcher at NECSTLab, Politecnico di Milano, working on stress detection from wearable data, with two IEEE papers so far.

Now · Zurich, Switzerland

Master's thesis at IDUN Technologies.

IDUN is an ETH Zurich spin-off building in-ear EEG: brain sensing from inside the ear canal. I do the data science behind their proprietary algorithms for estimating mental state from brain signals.

  • In-ear EEG

    Brain activity recorded where earbuds already sit. No cap, no gel.

  • Mental-state algorithms

    Models that map neural activity to cognitive and affective states.

  • Data science

    Signal processing, feature engineering and rigorous validation on real-world recordings.

The heart keeps a diary.

Published · IEEE EMBC 2026

A mental-stress index from a physiological foundation model.

I fine-tuned NormWear, a foundation model for wearable time series, on laboratory data, and added a regression head that predicts stress levels against validated psychometric questionnaires. The architecture is designed for federated deployment, so raw data never has to leave the device.

MIMIC-IV

Clinical phenotypes from ICU time series.

Variational autoencoders compress high-dimensional physiological recordings from the MIMIC-IV database into embeddings. Unsupervised clustering then reveals distinct clinical phenotypes.

VAEClusteringRepresentation learning
